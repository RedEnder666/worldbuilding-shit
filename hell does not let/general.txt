A dynamic FPS shooter against nazis and hell and zombie nazis and demon nazis, all under the watchful eye of the literal old testament god about to unmake the entire area you are in.

The protagonist, "The Unseen", is a faceless, nameless Belarusian anarchist with a death wish. Believes his actions are meaningless, but nazi suffering worths living a little longer.


The protagonist doesn't understand German. So the Nazi radio chatter, their screams, their commands—they are all garbled, distorted, and filtered, but also the visuals - things that are written, said, or named - be it un UI, the game docs, or textures, are his interpretations, not objective reality. It's not a translation from German, it's a text in german, but it's not the real text it's supposed to say - it's opinionated nonsense he assumes it says.
For example:
he sees a sign in german, and it says "Ein blödes Nazi-Schild mit Nazi-Scheiße, die du nicht verstehen willst!"
in objective reality it may say "Rauchen verboten. Bereich der Höllenpforten!" it's not just general nonsense, it's his opinion of Nazis - every german text in the game is a vulgar joke against Nazis.

THE CONFLICT:
Nazi "Höllearschbacken" Project: Using occult science, they've learned to open temporary portals to a demonic dimension, using it as a bunker to survive the erasure.

The Unseen's "Grace Shield": An unexplained metaphysical phenomenon. The sheer, focused act of destroying this specific evil generates a temporary field of "significance" that even God's fire cannot touch. It's not faith; it's action that creates salvation. It was always in the universe, even god didn't actually know because the Grace Shield came first.

The game should have less jumping, somehow, but it should be fast-paced. You're running, shooting, hiding, running, shooting. Not jumping over their heads doing a 180 mid-flight, but also not sitting behind a rock for hours. It should feel like half life with less high-rate-of-fire guns and more overpowered high caliber rifles and something like a normal mosin. Pistols should be useful against demons. Maps should be semi-open - you can see the sky, but you're generally in a town or a city, not an open field or a forest. It should have some basements, too, and the most open levels are inside of hell itself. Brutal TTK, but you're also easy to die. The enemies should miss often when they're operating automatic weapons, so you feel lucky and not like a bullet sponge. Ultimately the game should have nazis losing limbs, blood spilling, and all that.

Divine wrath comes as the end of the level, after which you appear in the same level, except destroyed like after a nuke, and everything is dark, empty. No enemies, no NPCs, no music. You can only see a portal you walk into, which gets you to the next level.
