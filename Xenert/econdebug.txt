Guaranteed Necessities (food, water, shelter, healthcare) are unconditional and free. You can't create a black market for something everyone already has.
BSG Points: Cannot be traded; can only be converted to personal DG points. Removes profit motive for bootlegging restricted goods.
DG Points: Can be gifted/traded, but are erased monthly. You cannot save, hoard, or invest them. Large bribes must be spent immediately on luxury goods, making corruption blatantly obvious.
No liquid foreign currency exists. The Concordian Dollar has no domestic use. It's a useless collectible, not a functional shadow currency.
Council positions are short-term and rotated. No individual has unilateral power to grant major favors (like housing). Corruption would require bribing an entire rotating committee simultaneously—a near-impossible, high-risk conspiracy.
Housing logs are public, cooperative ledgers are open. In tight-knit communes, unexplained lavish consumption would be instantly noticed and met with devastating social ostracism, which is a worse punishment than legal penalty in a societist society.




"We conducted an expansive research regarding historical housing crises and homelessness, and the data concluded something phenomenal. You can fit and live inside one house" - Representative of the USCE at an economic forum in Concordia, 2015
