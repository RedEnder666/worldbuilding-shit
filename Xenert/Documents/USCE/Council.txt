COMMONS COORDINATION BOARD, 2012:
The Anarcho-Cooperativist Party (ACP) (24 seats – Largest Bloc)
Ideology: "No gods, no masters, especially no centralized bureaucrats."
The biggest party actively works to make sure nobody rules.
They’re the reason the Commons Board can’t become a traditional state and stays mostly a lawmaker, and, rarely, an edge-case temporary ruler.
They don’t want to govern—they’re just here to veto any attempt at centralization.

Democratic-Societist (DSP) + Socialist Democratic (SDP) Coalition  (12 + 12 = 24 seats)
"Art, drugs, and vibes are human rights."
Ensures cultural production gets funding, artists get points, and nobody gets purged for being weird.
And they're pretty moderate actually. Everybody likes them.


Liberal-Societist (LSP) + SocDem (SocDP) Coalition (8 + 6 = 14 seats)
"Markets can be fun if nobody starves!"
Pushes for tradable discretionary points (but only for luxuries, never survival goods).
Seek to abolish Body and Behavioral Stability Goods category and include it in Discretionary Goods to allow a freer market. 


Party of The Military Servicemen and Patriotic Duty (MSPD) (4 seats)
"We won the revolution, now give us nice parks and public transit."
 The veterans' party. They don’t care about ideology—they just want urban planning done right.
Their goal: Every street needs at least 5 green zones. 1 to separate lanes on the road, 2 between cars and pedestrians(1 per side), 2 between pedestrians and buildings(1 per side).
