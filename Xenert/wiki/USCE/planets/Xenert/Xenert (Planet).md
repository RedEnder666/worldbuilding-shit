  **Xenert** is the third planet in its planetary system and a terrestrial body within the circumstellar habitable zone. It is the only known planet to support liquid water and a nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere conducive to complex life. The planet is characterized by active plate tectonics, a robust hydrologic cycle, and significant climactic and ecological diversity across its surface.

## Physical Characteristics

Xenert has a single natural satellite, **[[Lunara]]**, which exerts a significant gravitational influence on its tides. The planet's rotational axis is tilted relative to its orbital plane, resulting in pronounced seasonal variations across most of its latitudinal range.

- **Mean Radius:** 6,371 km
- **Surface Area:** 510 million km²
- **Landmass Area:** 149 million km² (29.2%)
- **Hydrosphere:** 361 million km² (70.8%)
- **Orbital Period (Xenertian Year):** 372.5 local days
- **Rotational Period (Xenertian Day):** 26 hours

## Geography

The planetary surface is divided into several major continental landmasses and extensive oceanic systems.

**Continents:**
*   **[[Entantha]]:** A large continent in the western hemisphere, featuring a massive central plain, the rugged Vesterna Uplands, and a complex, fractured eastern coastline.
*   **[[North Nisia]]:** A continent in the northern mid-latitudes, characterized by vast temperate forests and major river basin systems.
*   **[[South Nisia]]:** A continent spanning equatorial and southern latitudes, dominated by arid interior plains and dense equatorial jungles.
*   **[[Fakhra]]:** A largely frozen landmass centered on the southern rotational pole, dominated by glacial ice sheets.
*   **[[Kanalla]]:** A small, geologically stable continent in the western ocean, known for its topographical consistency.

## Atmosphere and Climate

Xenert's atmosphere is composed primarily of Nitrogen (~78%) and Oxygen (~21%), with trace amounts of other gases. This composition supports a wide range of climate zones, from hyper-arid deserts to humid tropical regions and polar ice caps. The planet is currently in an interglacial period.
