The **Union of Societist Communes of Entantha (USCE)** is a political entity located on the continent of [[Entantha]], founded in the aftermath of the [[Sechian Civil War (1901-1911)]]. It operates on principles of cooperative ownership, direct democracy, and equitable resource allocation, forming a distinct **societist** system.

## History

* **1903:** [[Declaration of the Communes of Entantha (1903) | Declaration of the Communes]]
* **1911:** Collapse of the Sechian Empire
* **1919:** [[Declaration of Independence (1919) | Formal Declaration of Independence]] from Sechia

## Governance

The USCE is governed by the **[[Commons Coordination Board]]**, a legislative body composed of multiple political parties. Key governing documents include:

* [[Declaration of the Communes of Entantha (1903)]]
* [[Declaration of Independence (1919)]]
* [[USCE Legal Framework]]
## Economic System

The USCE employs a unique **societist** economic model based on:

* **[[Resource Allocation Regulation]]** - Personal resource point system
* **[[Cooperatives Regulation]]** - Worker-owned productive units
