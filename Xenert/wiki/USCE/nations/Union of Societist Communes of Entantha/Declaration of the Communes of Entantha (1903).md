The **Declaration of the Communes of Entantha**, commonly known as the **Declaration of the Communes**, was a foundational document ratified on July 7, 1903, during the [[Sechian Civil War (1901-1911)]]. It formally announced the secession of Western Entantha from the Sechian Empire and established the philosophical basis for the [[Union of Societist Communes of Entantha|USCE]].

## Historical Context

The declaration was a direct product of the escalating [[Sechian Civil War (1901-1911)]]. The war, which began in 1901 as a schism within the Imperial military (the "Warriors' State"), created a power vacuum and opportunity for long-suppressed revolutionary movements. In Western Entantha, years of economic exploitation and theocratic oppression under the Sechian Empire had fostered a robust network of communes and cooperatives, which saw the Empire's distraction as their moment to strike.

Drafted and ratified in secret by a "Congress of Western Communes" in the city of Arsis, the declaration was both a strategic political act and a revolutionary manifesto. Its publication marked the USCE's formal entry into the civil war as a third belligerent, transforming the conflict from a two-way imperial struggle into a three-way ideological war.

## Content and Philosophical Foundations

The declaration is structured as a direct refutation of Sechian theocratic doctrine and a positive assertion of **societist** principles.

### Key Themes Include:

*   **Anti-Theocracy:** The document systematically rejects divine right and ecclesiastical authority, accusing the Empire of using religion as "a leash for the masses." It famously states, "We are not its flock."
*   **Popular Sovereignty:** It asserts that "all power derives from the commons, not from crowns nor clergy," establishing the people—organized in communes—as the only legitimate source of authority.
*   **Egalitarianism:** It commits to a society where "no birth defines a man" and pledges to build a union "not of rulers, but of federated cooperatives."
*   **Historical Grievances:** It lists specific charges against the Empire, including the burning of books, the suppression of reason, economic neglect, and violent repression of dissent.

### The Societist Compact

Unlike a traditional constitution establishing a state, the declaration frames the new USCE as a "Union... of federated cooperatives" bound by "conscience and consensus." This reflects the core societist ideal of a horizontal, cooperative commonwealth as a radical alternative to both monarchy and liberal republicanism.

## Legacy

The Declaration of the Communes is considered the birth certificate of the USCE. Its ratification date (July 7) is celebrated as Union Day, the nation's most important public holiday. The document's closing motto—"**We simply are**"—was adopted as the official motto of the USCE, encapsulating the movement's assertion of its own legitimacy and existence without recourse to higher authority.

It remains a sacred text within the USCE, frequently invoked in political discourse and legal proceedings, and serves as the preamble to the nation's foundational legal code, the [[USCE Legal Framework]].

> [!abstract]- Text of the Declaration
> **Declaration of the Communes of Entantha**  
> *In Congress, July 7th, 1903*
> 
> When a people, long divided by chains, rises as one — those once enslaved, and those once complicit — to reclaim the land from tyranny and lies, it becomes their sacred right and solemn duty to declare the causes which impel them to unity.
> 
> We hold these truths to be proven:  
> That liberty is not granted from pulpits nor palaces;  
> That all power derives from the commons, not from crowns nor clergy;  
> That no person is born to rule over another.  
> The Empire of Sechia, under which we have suffered has forsaken its duty to its subjects and its soul. It has scorched knowledge from libraries. It has decreed blind obedience in place of reason. It has yoked the divine as a leash for the masses, and in its silence toward our pleas, revealed its contempt.
> 
> It burned books and spared sermons.  
> It fed temples while villages starved.  
> It crowned madmen and called it holy.  
> It governed from above and listened never.  
> It chained the innocent in body and thought, sanctifying slavery in the name of gods who never spoke  
> And when we rose in voice, it met us with steel and flame.  
> No more.
> 
> We, the united communes of Entantha — native and settler, worker and thinker — declare ourselves severed from all bonds to the Imperial Throne. We are not its colony. We are not its flock. We are free people.
> 
> We establish the Union of Societist Communes of Entantha, not as a nation of rulers, but of federated cooperatives; not under gods, but under conscience and consensus. Let the new standard be equity. Let the new law be collective.  
> Let no tyrant rise again.  
> Let no man starve again.  
> Let no birth define a man again.  
> We do not ask permission.  
> We do not await blessing.  
> We simply are.
> 
> In unity,  
> For the victims of the past.  
> For the children of the Future.
