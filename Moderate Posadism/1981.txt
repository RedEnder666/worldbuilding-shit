The Domino Falls

“…and with her died my last warm feelings for humanity.”
— J.V. Stalin, 1932

# Stalin's worst nightmare. Reformism.

**1962:** Argentine Trotskyist visionary J. Posadas, in a moment of profound crisis, rejects his own doctrine of nuclear annihilation. His "Second Manifesto" argues the true catalyst for global communism is not atomic war, but the liberation of all knowledge and contact with advanced extraterrestrial socialism. "Mindful Posadism" is born—a creed of desperate, cosmic hope.

**1963:** 
- On November 22, in Dallas, Texas, a sequence of shots ring out from the Texas School Book Depository as President Kennedy's motorcade passes. History fractures on a centimeter's difference. The first shot misses. The second, fired as the limousine turns onto Elm Street, strikes the President in the upper right shoulder, passing through without hitting bone or major arteries. A third shot goes astray. President survives. After emergency surgery, a pale and pain-etched John F. Kennedy appears before the nation from his hospital room. His voice is weakened but his eyes hold a fire no one had seen before. He states, calmly, that the problem is not the left or the right - it is the radicalism that has poisoned both. And he will fight for human rights against all radicalism - right and left.

**1964:** 
- Leonid Brezhnev chokes to death during a New Year's breakfast. The conservative coup against Nikita Khrushchev falters before it begins.
- In the US, the Civil Rights Act passes. Kennedy argues "This law is the antidote to the radical poison. It is the rational, just solution that denies extremists on all sides the chaos they feed on."

**1965-1970:** The **Khrushchev-Kosygin Continuum** solidifies. De-Stalinization becomes irreversible. The Kosygin Reforms transform the Soviet economy into a hybrid model of planned efficiency and market-style responsiveness. "Peaceful Coexistence" is a doctrine of confident competition, not weakness.

**1971:** An aging Khrushchev retires with state honors. **Alexei Kosygin** assumes the role of General Secretary. He appoints the young reformer **Mikhail Gorbachev** as Premier.

**1972:** The ideology of Mindful Posadism resonates deeply with the Aymara and Quechua miners. The POR-TP(Partido Obrero Revolucionario Trotskista Posadista) transforms from a small Trotskyist party into a mass Indigenous-Posadist Movement.

**1973:** In Chile, General Augusto Pinochet perishes in a freak helicopter accident during his own coup attempt. The socialist government of Salvador Allende survives, stabilizes, and becomes a democratic socialist beacon.

**1968-1977:** The **Mexican Civil War**. Government collapse leads to a three-way fracture:
*   The **Mexican Free State**, a US-backed fascist rump in the north.
*   The **Republic of Mexico**, a fragile, neutral remnant clinging to Mexico City.
*   The **Mayab Minds' Republic**, a Trotskyist-Posadist state born from an alliance of Maya rebels and intellectuals in the south, preaching the liberation of knowledge and cosmic outreach. Their heroes are Zapata, Trotsky, Posadas, and various ufologists.

**1976:** Luna mission - Soviet Space Program lands a crewed mission on the surface of the Moon. Alexei Leonov becomes the first Soviet man on the Moon, and Valery Bykovsky becomes the second loneliest man in space history(as the orbital commander). What the US claimed as the victory, was just the first act.

**1977-1979:** In Bolivia, a brutal government crackdown on a miners' strike in 1977 sparks a nationwide uprising. The POR-TP, now allied with militant campesino militias and radicalized factions of the military, inspired by the Mayab Minds' Republic, launches a coordinated insurrection. People's Assembly of Workers, Peasants, and Soldiers, dominated by the POR-TP, declares the Socialist Republic of the Andean Cosmos (República Socialista del Cosmos Andino).

**1979:** The USSR, pragmatic and wary of quagmires, opts for massive proxy support to the Parcham faction in Afghanistan's civil war. It does not invade. The Cold War gains a new, grinding frontline.

**1980:** 
- A Mayab astrophysicist, aided by Soviet *Interkosmos* program, becomes the Republic's first citizen in space. The launch is a ideological thunderclap.
- USSR, under the GLASNOST policy of Kosygin and Gorbachev, publicly states the development of OGAS network, using the Akademset infrastructure, is already in progress. "In the end, communism is about precision. OGAS provides that precision. It replaces the fog of the plan with the clarity of the algorithm." — General Secretary Kosygin Alexei Nikolayevich.
- Soviet Space Program gets its name "SOVKOSMOS", Pravda Newspaper is now the official source of information for the plans and ambitions of USSR in space.

**1981:**
- "We will not match their five-year plans with our own. We will drown their network with a thousand-... no, a million of our own making, in garages, in universities, in free association." — President Frank Forrester Church III
- The Cold War turns into "Cold Race" - the superpowers trying to one-up eachother in progress. International Pact is an interesting, but minor faction, seen as a poison for the Moscow's well.
- Head of Sovkosmos, Anatoly Cherdenko, during an interview, said: "We're looking at the one place that hasn't been corrupted by capitalism... SPACE!"
